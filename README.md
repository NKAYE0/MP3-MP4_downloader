# Media Downloader

> ⚠️ **Note:** This project is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, YouTube Music, TikTok, or their respective owners.

## 🔎 What is this?

A feature-rich free, open-source media downloader with three dedicated sections:

* ▶️ **YouTube** — Download videos, playlists, and thumbnails
* 🎵 **YouTube Music** — Download songs, albums, playlists, and album artwork
* 🎵 **TikTok** — Download TikTok videos and audio

## ✨ Features

* 🎬 MP3 & MP4 downloads
* 📋 Full YouTube playlist downloads
* 🖼️ YouTube video thumbnails
* 🎵 YouTube Music album & playlist support
* 🎨 Album artwork downloads
* 🚫 **SponsorBlock integration** — optionally remove sponsored segments from downloaded videos
* 🖱️ **Drag & drop URLs**
* 📋 **Clipboard detection** — automatically detect copied URLs
* ▶️ **Built-in media player**
* 🕘 **Search & download history**
* 📊 **Download statistics**
